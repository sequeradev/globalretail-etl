## Insight 1 — Revenue is heavily UK-concentrated

The United Kingdom alone accounts for **82.0%** of total revenue (€8,550,818). The next 5 countries combined (Netherlands, EIRE, Germany, France, Australia) only contribute €1,319,064 (12.7%).

> **Implication:** any UK-specific risk (Brexit, tax change, currency shock) is an existential risk for the business. International expansion is not a growth lever — it's a survival hedge.

## Insight 3 — Revenue concentration in a few high-value customers

The top 10 customers (out of 4,338) generate **17.3%** of revenue. The top 1% of customers (43 accounts) generate **31.8%**.

> **Implication:** churn of even a single top-10 account is a measurable hit to revenue. Account-management investment for these customers has an outsized ROI.

## Insight 4 — Customer retention is the real growth lever

**34.4%** of customers placed only one order in the period; only **65.6%** came back. Given the revenue concentration above, converting first-time buyers into repeat buyers is mathematically more valuable than acquiring new ones.
